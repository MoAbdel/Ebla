Our Heritage

5,000 years of pressing.
Zero shortcuts.

The ancient city of Ebla — modern-day Tell Mardikh, Syria — was pressing olives before most civilizations had a written language. Our name is our promise: the same soil, the same craft, the same reverence for the olive that has defined this land for millennia.

We work exclusively with growers who have tended the same groves for generations. Every bottle is traceable to its grove, its harvest, its press date.
